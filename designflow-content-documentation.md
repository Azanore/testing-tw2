### 12. FAQ CONTENT

#### Questions & Answers

1. **Q:** "How does DesignFlow integrate with existing design tools?"
   **A:** "DesignFlow connects directly with Figma, Sketch, and Adobe XD through official plugins. Changes in your design files automatically sync to your design system, keeping everything up to date."

2. **Q:** "Can I export components for multiple platforms?"
   **A:** "Yes, DesignFlow generates production-ready code for React, Vue, Angular, React Native, Flutter, iOS (SwiftUI), and Android. All from a single design system source."

3. **Q:** "What happens to my data if I cancel my subscription?"
   **A:** "You retain full access to export your design systems and generated code. We provide a 30-day grace period to download all your data before account closure."

4. **Q:** "How does version control work for design systems?"
   **A:** "DesignFlow uses Git-like versioning. You can create branches, merge changes, and roll back to previous versions. Each change is tracked with timestamps and author information."

5. **Q:** "Is there a limit on team members or projects?"
   **A:** "The Professional plan includes unlimited team members and design systems. The Starter plan is limited to 1 design system and basic features for individual use."

6. **Q:** "Do you offer enterprise security features?"
   **A:** "Yes, Enterprise plans include SSO, SAML authentication, audit logs, custom security policies, and the option for on-premise deployment."
